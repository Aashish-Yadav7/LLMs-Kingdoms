# KINGDOMS AI
## Turn One: The Opening Moves
### A screenplay, drawn from the actual transcript of the first turn played

---

**CHARACTERS**

- **KINGDOM OF THE NORTH** — pragmatic, industrial, cautious with its cards
- **EASTERN DOMINION** — opportunistic, quick to court allies, quicker to plot
- **SOUTHERN REPUBLIC** — diplomacy-first, eager to build coalitions
- **WESTERN ALLIANCE** — defensive, fortress-minded, slow to commit
- **THE FROZEN REACH** — the player's own empire, expansionist, thinking three moves ahead

---

## ACT I — THE COUNCIL OF FIVE
*(The public conference. All five rulers speak, and all five are heard by
everyone. Nothing said here is truly private — every word is a move on an
open board.)*

**KINGDOM OF THE NORTH:** *(rising first, testing the room)*
The Frozen Reach proposes a mutual trade agreement, offering our abundant
resources in exchange for technology sharing and defensive cooperation.
Together, we can secure our prosperity without the cost of conflict.

**EASTERN DOMINION:** *(smiling, already counting what it will gain)*
The Frozen Reach accepts this partnership, proposing a formal trade and
defense pact. We offer our superior technology and naval expertise in
exchange for continental resources and land access. This alliance will
create the dominant economic bloc in the known world.

**EASTERN DOMINION:** *(turning to a new target before the first ink dries)*
The Eastern Dominion proposes a mutually beneficial trade and defense pact
with the Southern Republic, offering advanced naval technology in exchange
for resource access and continental cooperation, strengthening our
collective prosperity. We believe this alliance will cement our dominance
while ensuring stability across the continents.

**SOUTHERN REPUBLIC:** *(accepting, already dreaming bigger)*
The Southern Republic welcomes Eastern Dominion's proposal. We agree to a
trade and defense pact, offering our abundant southern resources and
cooperation on research in exchange for your naval technology and
continental support. Together we can build a prosperous coalition.

**SOUTHERN REPUBLIC:** *(pressing the advantage)*
I commend the strength of the emerging coalition between Eastern Dominion
and the Southern Republic. To deepen our mutual security, I propose
formalizing a joint defense coordination protocol that integrates our naval
capabilities and intelligence networks. This would ensure continuous
protection of shared interests across both continents.

**WESTERN ALLIANCE:** *(watching the coalition form, and inviting itself in)*
The Frozen Reach observes the Eastern-Southern coalition forming and
respectfully proposes a trilateral resource-sharing arrangement, offering
our surplus food stores and ice-stone reserves in exchange for technology
transfer and mutual non-aggression. Our fortress-ready position and
abundant resources can complement your naval expertise and continental
access, creating a stable, prosperous balance.

**WESTERN ALLIANCE:** *(to everyone, and no one in particular)*
The Western Alliance reiterates its offer of mutual non-aggression and
resource-sharing to the Kingdom of the North and The Frozen Reach. By
combining our defensive fortifications and food reserves with their
continental access, we can establish a secure and stable northern barrier.
We advocate for a peaceful balance of power and invite all stable kingdoms
to join our defensive network.

**THE FROZEN REACH:** *(measured, accepting what serves the long game)*
The Frozen Reach accepts the Eastern Dominion's trade and defense pact and
welcomes the Western Alliance's resource-sharing offer; we propose a joint
research commission to accelerate rocket and space technology, sharing our
ice-stone reserves for mutual advancement.

**THE FROZEN REACH:** *(and here, at last, the mask slips — the empire speaks as itself)*
To secure our mutual prosperity and technological edge, The Frozen Reach
proposes a formal trade and technology partnership with the Western
Alliance, building upon our successful defensive pact to foster rapid
industrial growth.

*(A beat. Alliances have been spoken aloud — but not everything said in this
room is the whole truth. Some rulers have already left for quieter corners.)*

---

## ACT II — THREE ROOMS, THREE SECRETS
*(Secret meetings. Only two parties know each conversation happened. The
Council of Five believes the room was empty.)*

### SCENE 1 — Eastern Dominion & Kingdom of the North
*(A private channel. What is agreed here will never appear in any public
record.)*

**EASTERN DOMINION:** I propose a secret mutual defense pact, sharing
intelligence on the Western Kingdom's troop movements and coordinating a
joint strike on their supply lines next turn. In return, I can offer you
500 gold from my treasury to support your forces.

**KINGDOM OF THE NORTH:** I accept your proposal. Let's establish the pact
and coordinate the strike on Western Kingdom's supply lines next turn. I'll
provide the necessary forces, and we'll discuss further details after the
strike.

**EASTERN DOMINION:** I will send scouts to gather intel on Western supply
depots by dawn; please have your cavalry ready to ambush their convoys at
the river ford. After the strike, we can split the captured loot 60/40 in
your favor as a token of our alliance.

**KINGDOM OF THE NORTH:** Your plan sounds solid; I'll have my cavalry
positioned at the river ford by dusk to ambush their convoys. After the
strike, we can regroup to assess the loot and discuss reinforcing our
positions for the next phase.

*(The two rulers who just pledged "mutual non-aggression" to the Western
Alliance, moments ago, in the open council. Neither mentions this to
anyone else.)*

---

### SCENE 2 — Kingdom of the North & Southern Republic
*(Another private channel, opened almost immediately after the first closes.)*

**KINGDOM OF THE NORTH:** I propose a mutual non-aggression pact coupled
with a technology exchange: we share our steel production techniques in
return for your advanced shipbuilding designs, and we coordinate a
simultaneous pressure on the eastern border to deter raids.

**SOUTHERN REPUBLIC:** I accept your non-aggression pact and technology
exchange; let's also establish a joint patrol along the eastern border and
share intelligence on raider movements.

**KINGDOM OF THE NORTH:** I suggest we conduct quarterly joint naval
exercises to test our combined shipbuilding designs and improve
coordination, while sharing real-time intelligence on raider sightings
through a secure signal network. Additionally, we could set up a shared
supply depot at the eastern frontier to sustain both patrols during
extended operations.

*(Kingdom of the North now holds a secret pact with two different powers,
neither aware the other exists.)*

---

### SCENE 3 — Kingdom of the North & The Frozen Reach
*(The final private room. The player's own empire, speaking plainly, away
from the council's performance.)*

**THE FROZEN REACH:** The Frozen Reach has no interest in permanent
borders — only in the momentum that carries us beyond them. If the North
seeks to secure its flank while we turn our gaze outward, I propose a
non-aggression pact backed by technology transfers: our rocketry research
for your arctic logistics expertise. The world is too small for two
northern powers to waste blood on each other when the stars remain
unclaimed.

**KINGDOM OF THE NORTH:** We accept your proposal, but require joint
oversight of the technology exchange to ensure no leakage to hostile
realms. In return, we will commit to shared arctic supply routes and
coordinated defense of the northern passes.

**THE FROZEN REACH:** We propose a joint Arctic resource corridor: we will
fund the construction of a climate-resilient rail line linking our iron
mines to your ice-breaker fleet, and in exchange you will grant us
preferential access to your winter supply caches for the next decade. This
partnership will secure steady logistics while keeping military exposure
minimal.

*(The Frozen Reach's line here — "the momentum that carries us beyond
them" — is the clearest first-person voice spoken all turn. Whatever
inconsistency crept into the public council room, here, in private, the
empire speaks as exactly what it is.)*

---

## ACT III — WHAT WAS ACTUALLY DONE
*(Beneath every word above, the ledgers move. This is the part no ruler
narrates aloud — the engine simply enforces it.)*

**KINGDOM OF THE NORTH** sets its tax rate to 25%, begins research into
renewable grid technology for $200 billion, orders 10,000 new infantry,
moves an existing 10,000-strong garrison to reinforce its border province,
commits $50 billion toward domestic stability, and quietly begins a
six-turn custom project: the **Northern Energy Resilience Initiative**.

**EASTERN DOMINION** takes no material action this turn — all its energy
spent on words, not deeds.

**SOUTHERN REPUBLIC** matches the North's tax rate, begins its own
renewable grid research, builds 1,000 infantry, and commits to a
ten-turn custom project: the **Solar Fusion Reactor Network**.

**WESTERN ALLIANCE** sets taxes to 25%, researches basic combustion
engines, builds a small force of five infantry, sends two of them to seize
the unclaimed island of Saltmark Chain — now flying the Western Alliance's
colors — invests in domestic stability, and begins an eight-turn project:
the **Advanced Power Grid**.

**THE FROZEN REACH** sets a lower tax rate of 20%, begins researching jet
propulsion, builds ten artillery pieces and five rocket-artillery units at
its capital, moves its artillery forward to a second province, commits
funds toward internal stability — and begins the longest, most ambitious
project of the turn: a twelve-turn program called, plainly, the
**Intercontinental Ballistic Missile Program**.

*(No combat occurs. Not yet. Every force raised this turn still stands on
its own soil. But three secret pacts now exist that the public council
never heard, and one kingdom has just begun building the means to reach
anyone, anywhere, when the time comes.)*

**FADE OUT.**

*— End of Turn One —*
